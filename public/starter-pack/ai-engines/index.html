AI engine pricing

Every Starter Pack client picks an AI engine at setup. Your hosting plan includes a monthly AI-usage allowance (Essentials $10 · Growth $25 · Scale $60 of provider usage). Pricier engines use that allowance faster; nothing is ever charged beyond your plan — when the allowance is used up, AI replies pause until the next billing period, and we tell you at 80%, 95% and 100%.

Things worth knowing

  • You can change engines later from your dashboard; your AI is rebuilt with the new models in a few minutes.
  • Every engine runs on our own Azure deployments — your conversations and documents never go to a third-party API.
  • Only engines that are fully deployed on our platform are offered in the picker; if you don't see one listed here, it's on its way.
  • Questions about volume pricing or a dedicated deployment? Talk to us.
