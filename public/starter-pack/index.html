What does monthly hosting cover?

Every plan includes hosting, maintenance, usage tracking, and advance limit warnings. Essentials is $50/month, Growth is $100/month, and Scale is $200/month. Each step adds more storage, website-assistant replies, Voice AI minutes, and Private AI processing. Your selected plan appears in Stripe checkout: its first month is charged today with your product purchase, then hosting renews monthly until canceled.

What happens if I reach a monthly limit?

Only the feature that reached its limit pauses. Your other products and stored data stay available. You can wait for the next monthly reset or choose a larger fixed plan in your Stripe billing portal. There are no surprise per-message, per-minute, or storage overage charges from Felican AI.

Do I really own it?

Yes. The AI is trained on your business, branded as your business, and the knowledge base is yours. Your information isn't used to train a public model.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You paste your website link, drop in any documents you want it to know, and it builds itself. The website assistant is a single line of code your web person can paste in a minute — and if you don't have a web person, we'll walk you through it.
