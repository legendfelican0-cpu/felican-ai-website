ABOUT US

A family-built company that builds AI for a living

Felican AI is led by Lee Felican Jr. We focus on practical AI: products, custom systems, automation, integrations, implementation, and training.

Engineering first, AI second

Most AI work fails for unglamorous reasons: it never connects to the real systems, nobody owns it, and no one on staff was taught to run it. We come from thirty years of shipping software inside regulated industries, so those are the parts we take seriously.

We build products a business can switch on, and we build custom systems for companies whose process doesn't fit a product. Either way the goal is the same: something that still works six months after we hand it over.

Being family-built keeps us direct. You talk to the people who design and build the thing.

“Technology only matters when it changes the answer to a real question.”

Lee Felican Jr., founder
