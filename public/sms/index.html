SMS Terms & Opt-In

Effective September 17, 2026

Felican AI and the products we build and operate (including World of Agents, Felican Auto, COPS Private AI and Jarvis) can send you text messages — appointment and shift reminders, order and status updates, verification codes, alerts you asked for, and replies to texts you send us. This page explains how you opt in, what to expect, and how to stop at any time. It supplements our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Program description

Felican AI Notifications delivers transactional and informational text messages from a Felican AI phone number on behalf of the Felican AI product you use. We do not send marketing texts to anyone who has not specifically asked for them, and we never sell or rent your number.

How you opt in

You will only receive texts after you have agreed to them in one of these ways:

  • On a website or app form — by ticking the SMS consent box (unticked by default) and submitting your mobile number. The exact wording you agreed to, the time, and the page are recorded.
  • By replying YES to a one-time confirmation text that says who is asking and links to this page (double opt-in).
  • By texting us first — when you start a conversation with one of our numbers, we may reply to that conversation.
  • Verbally — when you tell a Felican AI team member or voice assistant that we may text you at a number you provide; the request is noted at that time.

Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase or service. You can always choose another contact method.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies by product and your activity — typically a few messages per week, more if you are actively texting with us. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message and you will be unsubscribed from all Felican AI texts immediately. You will get one final confirmation and nothing more. UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END and QUIT work the same way. To resubscribe, reply START. You can also ask us to stop at ai@felican.ai or +1 561-235-0799.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, email ai@felican.ai, or call +1 561-235-0799.

Privacy

Your mobile number, your opt-in record (how and when you agreed), and the messages exchanged are kept as business records so we can honor your choices and answer questions about them. Texting data — including consent and opt-out information — is not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing. See our Privacy Policy for how we protect information.

Supported carriers

Messages are delivered over iMessage and SMS to major U.S. carriers. Availability may vary by carrier and device.

“By checking this box and providing my mobile number, I agree to receive text messages from Felican AI (on behalf of the product I'm using) about my account and the services I've requested. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See felican.ai/sms and felican.ai/privacy.”
